About me

Building software that makes a difference

I'm a Senior Mobile Developer based in Paraguay with 4+ years of experience building fintech and enterprise mobile applications. I specialize in Flutter and clean architecture, focused on shipping maintainable, production-grade software at scale.

I've spent most of my career at e-tica, working on EKO and Banco Familiar — real-world banking apps used by thousands of users. I've grown from implementing features (30% fraud reduction, 2h → 5min identity lookups) to serving as TL Backup, standardizing mobile practices across squads. In between, I worked as Mobile Architect at itti, Paraguay's leading tech company, building KYC SDKs and driving clean architecture adoption across the mobile team.

Beyond my day job, I co-founded the IEEE Computer Science Society at Universidad Nacional de Asunción and served as its inaugural president. I'm passionate about open source, mentorship, and building things that last.
